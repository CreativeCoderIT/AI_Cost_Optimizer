# Go-To-Market Plan

The exact target user is a founder, engineering lead, or finance/operator at a 5-100 person startup that has started paying for multiple AI tools without a clear owner for AI spend. They are not yet big enough for a formal procurement team, but they are large enough that “everyone expensing AI tools” creates waste. The strongest buyer is probably a founder/CFO hybrid or Head of Engineering who sees AI spend rising across ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Copilot, and API usage.
Right before they want this tool, they are likely searching or scrolling for things like “Cursor pricing,” “ChatGPT Team worth it,” “Claude Team vs Pro,” “OpenAI API bill high,” or “how much should startups spend on AI tools.” They may also be reacting to a surprise credit card bill or a teammate asking to upgrade another AI subscription.
They hang out in founder and builder communities: X/Twitter startup circles, Indie Hackers, Hacker News, r/SaaS, r/startups, r/indiehackers, AI engineering Discords, Cursor/Claude/Copilot discussions, and Slack groups for founder communities or accelerators. On X, the best wedge is replying to posts where founders complain about AI tool sprawl or expensive API bills.

To get the first 100 users in 30 days with $0 paid budget, I would use a founder-led launch:
- Post a transparent build thread showing real before/after audits.
- Reply to AI cost complaint posts with a useful mini-audit, not a generic link.
- Share in Indie Hackers with the angle “I made a free AI subscription audit because teams are paying for overlapping tools.”
- DM 30 founders/operators with a short offer: “Send me your AI stack, I’ll run a free audit and show savings.”
- Ask each user with savings to share their public report screenshot.
- Write one practical post: “The 5 AI subscriptions startups overpay for first.”

The unfair distribution channel for Credex is that Credex can connect the audit to actual credit purchasing and vendor savings opportunities. Most calculators stop at advice. Credex can turn the highest-savings audits into consultation and purchase intent.

Week-1 traction target:
- 100 unique visitors
- 35 completed audits
- 10 email captures
- 3 high-savings consultation leads
- 2 real calls booked
