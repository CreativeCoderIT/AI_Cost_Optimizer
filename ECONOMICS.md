# Economics

This tool is valuable if it turns anonymous AI-spend curiosity into qualified Credex consultation leads. The rough unit economics depend on how much a converted lead is worth to Credex.

Assume a high-savings audit user spends $1,000-$10,000/month on AI tools or credits. If Credex can help them purchase credits, optimize vendor spend, or capture incentives, the value of one converted customer could reasonably be hundreds to thousands of dollars. For rough math, assume one converted Credex customer is worth $2,000 in gross contribution over the relationship. This is only an estimate, but it gives a working model.

Channel CAC with $0 paid budget:
- Founder DMs: $0 cash, high time cost. If 30 DMs produce 5 audits and 1 call, CAC is mostly labor.
- X/Twitter replies: $0 cash, medium time cost. If 20 useful replies produce 10 audits and 1 call, CAC is low.
- Indie Hackers/Hacker News posts: $0 cash, unpredictable. One strong post could produce 50-200 visitors.
- User-shared reports: $0 cash, strongest loop if public report screenshots are compelling.

Conversion assumptions:
- Visitor → completed audit: 25%
- Completed audit → email captured: 30%
- Email captured → consultation booked: 10%
- Consultation booked → credit purchase/customer: 25%

With 1,000 visitors:
- 250 completed audits
- 75 emails
- 7-8 consultations
- 2 customers

At $2,000 contribution per customer, 1,000 visitors could produce about $4,000 contribution. If the distribution is organic, that is attractive. If paid CAC rises above roughly $2-$4 per visitor, the funnel needs stronger conversion or higher customer value.

For this tool to drive $1M ARR in 18 months, Credex would need either high lead volume or much higher contract value. If one converted customer is worth $10k ARR, the tool needs 100 customers. At a 0.2% visitor-to-customer conversion rate, that means about 50,000 targeted visitors. If the tool improves to 1% visitor-to-customer through better qualification, follow-up, and high-intent distribution, it needs about 10,000 targeted visitors.

What must be true:
- AI spend continues growing inside startups.
- Founders/operators feel pain from overlapping AI subscriptions.
- Credex has a credible way to capture savings beyond simple plan downgrades.
- Public reports are shareable enough to create organic distribution.
- High-savings users trust the tool enough to book a consultation.
